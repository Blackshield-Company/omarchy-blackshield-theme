## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-17
### Added
- First release: colors.toml palette (16 ANSI colours, solid crimson `#c1121f` border, no gradient by design), eight backgrounds (2560x1440, `01-camp-vigil.png` is the default), four ultrawide (2560x1080) originals in `extras/ultrawide/`, btop theme, Omarchy shell and lock overrides (`shell.toml`, `shell.lock.toml`), lock-screen logo (`unlock.png`), `Yaru-red` icons, preview and screenshots.
